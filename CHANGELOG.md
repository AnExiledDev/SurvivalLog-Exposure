# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Settings changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen take effect at once.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer.

## 0.1.2

First public release. Slower exposure on expeditions: time exposure rises half as fast, running adds none, and searching and looting add half. Every amount can be set.
